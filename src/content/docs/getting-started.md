Choose a hero slot on the title screen and create your wanderer. Choose difficulty carefully: it can be raised later, but cannot be lowered in that world. [Read the difficulty rules](/mechanics/difficulty/) before choosing Hardcore.

1. Gather loose wood and stone around the homestead. Look for flint beside Stillwater Tarn, its brook, bog pools or the coast. Pick flax, moonpetals and redcaps when you find them; holding ingredients teaches their recipes.
2. Make a **Flint Axe** by hand (3 wood, 3 flint). It cuts birch and pine. A **Club** costs 5 wood.
3. A **Torch** costs 2 wood and 1 pine resin by hand. It lights the dark while held, wears down as it burns, and can be repaired at a workbench.
4. Build a workbench to craft a **Stone Pickaxe** (3 wood, 4 stone, 2 flint). Work towards better tools, weapons and protective gear.
5. Cook meat at a fire and carry healing supplies. Food improves your stamina and recovery; a healing draught restores health.
6. Build and claim your own bed under shelter; sleeping through the night grants Rested (from v0.24.0, furniture near
   the bed adds [comfort](/mechanics/building/#comfort) and makes it last longer). A chest costs 10 wood and no flint. Watch a creature's wind-up (ground attack warnings are off by default; Settings > Gameplay turns them on), save stamina for evasion (or block with a weapon), and return to prepare before pushing into dangerous regions.
7. When the satchel fills up, take what the land gives to **Orm the Peddler** by the Birchwood road, west of the old ruins. He pays in silver and sells a bigger satchel.

Your satchel holds **15 slots**, up to **20 of a kind in a slot**; spare gear uses one slot each. Worn gear and hotbar gear use no satchel slot. A bound consumable keeps one stack of up to 20 on the hotbar; further stacks use satchel space. What does not fit is set down at your feet, and loot on the ground waits until you have room. Orm sells more room, five slots at a time (30, 60, 100, 150 and 220 silver), up to 40.

What you gather grows back in its own time: a felled tree after about 7 minutes (once its log is cleared away), a broken rock after 8, a boulder after 12, an echo crystal after 10, and plants after about 5 to 20. Walking away and coming back does not hurry it, and nothing grows back while someone stands close by. On a server (from v0.23.0) the world remembers it for everyone, including players who join later.

If you are unsure what to do next, keep an eye on the quiet line your hero sometimes thinks out loud at the bottom of the screen.

Inventory and crafting screens leave the world running. Only the single-player pause menu pauses it; online co-op never pauses.
