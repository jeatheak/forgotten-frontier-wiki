The world spans a **720 m square**. The central Vale contains the homestead, forest and frontier; four outer regions blend into it. Since v0.19 the Vale is an **island**: the land runs down to a coast about 300 m from the middle (sandy beaches in the west and south, a marshy shore where the Mire meets the water, cliffs under the Stoneteeth) and the sea goes on to the horizon. You can wade out until the water reaches your chest; there the cold sea turns you back. Driftwood, rocks, sticks, pebbles and flint wash up on the beaches. (Before v0.19 a ring of mountains closed the edge.) Terrain and biome layout are fixed, while seeded nature placement varies between worlds.

The homestead is your base for crafting, storage and building. Follow paths into the forest, find places and lore, prepare for the northern frontier, and eventually reach the Forgotten Courtyard and Beacon. Barrows lead into underground crypts with their own dangers.

**Trader's Rest**, a stride off the Birchwood road west of the old ruins, is the camp of **Orm the Peddler**. He buys wood, stone, hides, ore, trophies and the outer lands' finds for silver, and sells a bigger satchel, healing draughts, arrows, whetstones, bandages and seed barley. Creatures do not hunt within his lantern light.

<div class="gallery-grid"><figure><a href="/images/trader-v017.webp" aria-label="Open full-size image: Orm the Peddler by his cart at dusk (v0.17.0)."><img src="/images/trader-v017.webp" alt="Orm the Peddler by his cart at dusk (v0.17.0)." width="1600" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a><figcaption>Orm the Peddler by his cart at dusk (v0.17.0).</figcaption></figure></div>

Lit signal fires are linked by the **fire paths**: throw a handful of resin on one and a column of flame carries you to another.
