**Story spoilers follow.**

The wanderer's journey is about making the world remember. Ruins hold Echoes of their former shapes; lore tablets, discoveries and conversations record fragments of the forgotten civilization. Read the in-game journal as you discover them.

Mira is an injured traveler who can become part of the homestead. She speaks everything she says aloud (Settings → Audio → Spoken dialogue turns her voice off), and tells her own story a chapter at a time as you see more of the Vale. The Ancient Guardian protects the courtyard on the approach to the Beacon; the Keepers sealed its gate, and the seal asks for proof (the outer lands walked, their trials passed, all companions recruited, a key forged from what each land gives) before it lets anyone in. Its defeat and the restoration of the Beacon complete the central journey, after which a hero may continue exploring or begin another saga.

Since v0.25.0 the Keepers left marks out on the Skerries too: a cairn on Gullholm and a lore stone by the ruined lighthouse on Keeper's Light, whose fire once guided their ships home. Kindling it again is a story beat of its own.

Since v0.28.0 the story reaches below the Vale. The Beacon's first fire was drawn up from **the Underhearth**, a world under the island with its own sky, and the Keepers built there too: the lift that hangs from an upside-down keep, the ward stones whose light no creature hunts in, and a great door sealed in the cliff between the Ashen Wilds and the Cinder Wastes. What the Keepers found down there, a light without a sun, is a mystery for later. See [the Underhearth](/world/underhearth/).

In the current source (v0.29.0, not released yet) four tablets in the Fallen Dominion tell the rest from the other side: the people of the Dominion came down from the cold and the deep gave them fire; they bound a light in the sky over their city; the Keepers came as guests, learned the fire, and carried the first fire up to their Beacon over the Dominion's answer, sealing the doors behind them.

This archive describes implemented story beats. It does not invent backstories or turn development plans into canon. Full tablet transcriptions and every discovery are not yet catalogued.
