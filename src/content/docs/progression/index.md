**Story and ending spoilers follow.**

Establish a home, craft tools and protective gear, and explore the forest and frontier. Discover lore and use Echoes to restore paths through the ruins. Find the injured **Mira**, clean her wound with a **Wound Salve** and bind it with a **Linen Bandage**, then invite her home. She teaches the **Riposte** (a parry strikes back at once) and enables the Ember forge rite.

Fighting teaches you too: **Dash Strike** is learned once any fighting skill reaches 10. The outer lands are each harder than the Vale and each has its own find; the Horn Bow needs spider silk from the Mire, the Amber Charm needs Elderwood amber. **Orm the Peddler** on the Birchwood road buys what you gather and sells a bigger satchel.

Prepare for the Forgotten Courtyard, defeat the Ancient Guardian and use the **Guardian's Heart** to restore the Beacon. Major restorations become permanent, and the game offers an ending choice.

## After the Beacon

Keep exploring your current Vale, or start a **new saga** in a fresh world. Restart at the same difficulty with a minor perk, or one step harder with a major perk. The Beacon offers this choice when revisited. In online co-op, begin new sagas in your hero's own Vale.

Perks accumulate across sagas, each once. Examples include extra health or stamina, faster movement, increased skill gain, better crafting and Second Wind. A set of three unowned perks is offered from the relevant pool.
