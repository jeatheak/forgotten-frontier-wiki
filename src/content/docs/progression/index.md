**Story and ending spoilers follow.**

Establish a home, craft tools and protective gear, and explore the forest and frontier. Discover lore and use Echoes to restore paths through the ruins. Find the injured **Mira**, clean her wound with a **Wound Salve** and bind it with a **Linen Bandage**, then invite her home. She teaches the **Riposte** (a parry strikes back at once) and enables the Ember forge rite.

Fighting teaches you too: **Dash Strike** is learned once any fighting skill reaches 10. The outer lands are each harder than the Vale and each has its own find; the Horn Bow needs spider silk from the Mire, the Amber Charm needs Elderwood amber. **Orm the Peddler** on the Birchwood road buys what you gather and sells a bigger satchel. From v0.25.0 a **coracle** from the workbench carries you out to **the Skerries**, four islands in the sea with sea chests, a Keepers' coffer and the lighthouse to kindle; none of it is needed for the Keepers' seal or the ending. See [the Skerries](/world/skerries/). In the current source (v0.28.0, not released yet) a fracture beside the Throat on the Highlands leads down into [the Underhearth](/world/underhearth/), where lighting the Ashen Wilds' two ward stones opens the way on to the Cinder Wastes; none of it is needed for the ending either.

## The Keepers' seal

<div class="gallery-grid"><figure><a href="/images/keepers-seal-v019.webp" aria-label="Open full-size image: The Keepers' seal on the Courtyard gate, every trial done (v0.19.0)."><img src="/images/keepers-seal-v019.webp" alt="A stone lore card titled The Keepers' Seal: a description of the sealed gate, the Keepers' words, and six trials each marked Done, with the choice to set the Keepers' Key in the seal." width="1600" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a><figcaption>The Keepers' seal on the Courtyard gate, every trial done (v0.19.0).</figcaption></figure></div>

Since v0.19 the **Courtyard gate is sealed**. The Keepers ask for proof before they let anyone at the Guardian; examine the seal at the gate's rune stone to see what is done and what is not:

1. Walk all four outer lands: Birchwood, the Mire, the Stoneteeth (Highlands) and Elderwood.
2. Break a barrow's seal: slay the Warden in the burial hall of a crypt.
3. Light a ruined watchtower's five fire baskets.
4. Wake the Gale relic in the shrine on the Mesa.
5. Forge the Ember relic.
6. Recruit every unlockable companion to the homestead (currently **Mira**).
7. Forge a **[Keepers' Key](/equipment/items/keepers-key/)** at the forge (it can be forged once the trials above are passed) from what only the outer lands give: amber, frost lichen, spider silk and cloudberries, with iron bars and echo crystals.

Set the key in the seal and the gate opens for good. In a world whose Guardian fell before v0.19, the gate is open.

## The Guardian and the Beacon

Defeat the Ancient Guardian in the Forgotten Courtyard and use the **Guardian's Heart** to restore the Beacon. Major restorations become permanent, and the game offers an ending choice.

## After the Beacon

Keep exploring your current Vale, or start a **new saga** in a fresh world. Restart at the same difficulty with a minor perk, or one step harder with a major perk. The Beacon offers this choice when revisited. In online co-op, begin new sagas in your hero's own Vale.

Perks accumulate across sagas, each once. Examples include extra health or stamina, faster movement, increased skill gain, better crafting and Second Wind (since v0.27.0 each hero's own, with the status line showing whether it is ready or spent until dawn). A set of three unowned perks is offered from the relevant pool.
