The current stable release is **v0.32.0 — real hands** (released with **v0.31.0 — in good stride**): hands with fingers
that grip what they hold, new walking, running and sneaking, and beds that need a sheltered room. The next version,
**v0.33.0 — the rune forge**, is documented ahead of its release: [two gear tiers above iron](/equipment/rune-forge/) for
the Underhearth, unlocked by the Guardian, the wards below and the Last Forge. **v0.30.0** brought [light to wear](/world/underhearth/#light-below)
in the Underhearth (a Glowcap Circlet, an Emberlit Lamellar and a Wisp Rune that floats over your shoulder), and ground
down there that grows: glow-threads under the glowcaps, ash and cinder reeds, pebbles and sprigs of glowing crystal.
**v0.29.0** led to [the Fallen Dominion](/world/underhearth/#the-fallen-dominion), a black city on an island in a lake
of fire, and **v0.28.0** opened [the Underhearth](/world/underhearth/), a second world under the Vale.
Build a home, brave the wilds, awaken Echoes and rekindle the Beacon.
