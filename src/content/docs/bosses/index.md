[Ancient Guardian: full statistics, attacks and drop table](/bosses/ancient-guardian/).

The Ancient Guardian waits in the **Forgotten Courtyard** and wakes when a hero moves inside its walls. Its attacks include a ground slam, wide sweep and committed charge.

Bait a charge into a wall or a restored Echo pillar to leave it stunned and **Exposed**. Use that opening, then save stamina for the next attack. Echoes help but are not required to win. Its later phase is more dangerous.

The definition starts at **1450 health** and **260 poise** before encounter modifiers, including difficulty and co-op scaling. Do not read these as guaranteed final fight values.

Its heart is part of [restoring the Beacon](/progression/). [Echo mechanics](/runes/) · [Difficulty](/mechanics/difficulty/).
