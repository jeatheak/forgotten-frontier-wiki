This is the player wiki for **The Forgotten Frontier**, a co-op survival and action RPG. It documents implemented game content. Current source version: **0.17.0 — wayfarers**. A source version does not prove that a release has been published.

Item, equipment and recipe references and the update archive are generated from game definitions. Player guides are reviewed against the relevant implementations. The maintenance workflow records source hashes and flags changed sources for review. It requires an agent working in the game project; it is not a background service.

## Pictures

The archive includes 96 real game icons (89 items and equipment, 7 stations), a collection of v0.16.0 photographs and v0.17.0 pictures of the new build panel, the trader, the guard, the fire paths and the outer lands' finds. Creature portraits and equipment demonstrations are staged using game tools, with their captions identifying that context. [Browse the image gallery](/gallery/). Historical captures are labeled with their versions.

## Coverage

The initial archive includes every stackable item, equipment definition and crafting recipe from the current source, plus guides to controls, survival, crafting, building, farming, difficulty, co-op, biomes, creatures, Echoes and progression. Creature references include attack values and drop probabilities; station references cover construction and processing. Equipment references include quality and upgrade scaling, with a shared calculator. Some discoveries and puzzle solutions remain uncatalogued. Do not infer missing values.

Story and progression pages contain spoilers. [Wiki source and corrections](https://github.com/jeatheak/forgotten-frontier-wiki).
